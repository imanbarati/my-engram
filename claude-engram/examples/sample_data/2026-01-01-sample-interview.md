# Sample interview (fictional)

Date: 2026-01-01
Topic: values
Language: both

Q: What do you value most in life?
A: [stated] Honesty and finishing what I start. I would rather do fewer things well than many things half-way. Family comes first when there is a real conflict.

Q: What advice would you give to someone younger?
A: [stated] Be patient with yourself. Learn by building real things, not just by reading. And protect your attention — it is the most scarce resource.

Q: What would you want people to remember about you?
A: [stated] That I tried to be useful and that I kept my word.
